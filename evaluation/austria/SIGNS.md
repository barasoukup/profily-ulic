# Austrian road signs: StVO 1960 §§ 50–54 and Commons SVG files

Research date: 2026-11-16. Nothing was written into any repository.

## Caveats (read first)

1. **Legal text version.** The RIS page fetched for § 52 (https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1960/159/P52/NOR12146646) is the version in force 1987–1989, marked as not current. The Z numbers below come from that text plus the Wikipedia sign table (https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bildtafel_der_Verkehrszeichen_in_%C3%96sterreich). Check the current StVO 1960 and Straßenverkehrszeichenverordnung (StVZVO) on RIS before using numbers in anything formal.
2. **Numbering.** The Wikipedia table says Austria has no official numbering system and that its numbers are its own. Commons file names use those numbers, and they match the StVO lit./Z system as far as I could check.
3. **Commons licence.** Every file checked carries the same line: "This road sign image is in the public domain according to the copyright law of Austria" (§ 7 Abs. 1 UrhG, amtliches Werk). No template code such as PD-AT-exempt was visible in the page text. The Commons category https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Road_signs_in_Austria holds only one SVG (Tierfabrik-Ende-Ortsschild.svg), so the category does not index the sign set.
4. **Not verified:** Radfahrstreifen sign, Mehrzweckstreifen sign, Straßenbahn lane sign, Haltestelle (bus stop) sign, Behindertenparkplatz sign, "ausgenommen Radfahrer" plate.

## Official templates and copying

- **Official source for the drawings:** the sign drawings are in the Straßenverkehrszeichenverordnung (StVZVO), which Commons cites as the basis for the PD licence. I did not fetch the StVZVO annex itself.
- **Copying:** the Commons licence says the signs are public domain under § 7 Abs. 1 UrhG (amtliche Werke; official works such as laws and ordinances are not copyright-protected in Austria). The Commons pages cite that basis, so copying the sign designs is generally allowed. This is a reading of the Commons statements and not legal advice; check § 7 UrhG (RIS) before publishing commercially.
- **Attribution:** not required by the PD line, but you may credit "Straßenverkehrszeichenverordnung" as the source.
